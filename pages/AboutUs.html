About Mangaung Digital Art Print

Welcome to Mangaung Digital Art Print.

A safe space and platform to help youth master digital skills and real-life experiences.

About us

Who We Are

Mangaung Digital Art Print is a registered community-focused Non-Profit Organisation located in Section A, Botshabelo.

Our Mission

Our primary focus is to drive youth empowerment and job creation within the Mangaung Metro Municipality.

Our Vision

To create an inclusive, technologically advanced creative hub in the Free State where media and the arts are the primary catalyst for change.

What we Focus On

  • Digital Skills Development: Equipping the youth with the knowledge to navigate modern software.
  • Professional Training: Giving our community a voice through digital art and photography.
  • Artistic Expression: Using graphic design and print technology to prepare our youth for the workplace.

Providing access to modern creative tools, training, and support for the youth of Botshabelo.

Our Services

  • Digital Art & Illustration
  • Photography & Visual Media
  • Graphic Design Essentials
  • Modern Print Technology
